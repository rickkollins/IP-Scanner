Workflow

Every operation is a feature in the history on the left. Double-click a feature to change it; the model rebuilds. Drag the blue rollback bar to view the model at an earlier step, and insert features there. Right-click for suppress, reorder and delete.

When a dialog has a Parts box, click parts in the view (or the parts list) to add or remove them.
